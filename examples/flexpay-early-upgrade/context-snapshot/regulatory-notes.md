# Regulatory prompts: UK handset financing

> Prompts for the PO to raise with Compliance. **Not legal advice**, and it may be out of date. Replace with your employer's own guidance when you have it.

Handset financing in the UK is usually regulated consumer credit, so these areas tend to come up:

| Area | What to ask about | Typical product impact |
|---|---|---|
| FCA Consumer Duty | Which of the four outcomes (products and services, price and value, consumer understanding, consumer support) does this change touch, and how will we evidence good outcomes? | Outcome metrics, testing of comms, monitoring by customer group |
| Creditworthiness and affordability (FCA CONC) | Does the journey create new credit or change existing credit? Does it need a new assessment? | Credit check step, data needed, decline journeys |
| Pre-contract information and explanations (Consumer Credit Act / CONC) | What must the customer see before they sign? | Content, document generation, audit trail |
| Early settlement | What must a settlement figure show, how quickly must a statement be provided on request, how long is a quote valid? | Settlement quote content, validity period, generation timing |
| Right of withdrawal | Can the customer withdraw from new credit, and how? | Post-sale journeys, comms |
| Vulnerable customers (FCA guidance) | How do we spot and support customers in vulnerable circumstances in a self-serve journey? | Signposting, routes to a human, no dark patterns |
| Credit reference searches | Hard or soft search, and does the customer know which? | Content, consent wording |
| UK GDPR | What personal data do we use, why, and for how long? | Data minimisation, privacy notice, retention |
| Accessibility | WCAG 2.2 AA is the default bar | Design and test effort |
| Complaints (DISP) | How do complaints about this journey get logged and routed? | Contact reasons, CRM tagging |

## How agents should use this file
- Raise relevant rows under "Regulatory and compliance considerations" as **questions for Compliance**, not as statements of law.
- Never tell the PO that something is compliant. Say what needs checking and who should check it.
