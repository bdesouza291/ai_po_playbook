# Brief: Self-serve early upgrade for FlexPlan customers

## The ask
Head of Digital Sales, in the Q4 planning session: "Mid-contract customers can't upgrade online. They ring us or they leave. Fix it."

## Why now
- Settlement figure calls are one of the top three contact reasons and rising.
- New flagship handsets launch in the spring; that's when early upgrade demand peaks.
- Strategy this year is to move routine servicing to self-serve.

## Who it's for
FlexPlan customers who are mid-contract (more than 3 months before the end) and want to upgrade or understand what they owe. Internal: contact-centre agents who handle these calls.

## What good looks like
More mid-contract customers upgrade online with us, and fewer of them need to call to get a settlement figure.

## Known constraints
- Anything showing balances or settlement figures needs Compliance sign-off.
- Credit Decisioning changes have a six-week vendor lead time, so I'd rather not touch them in the first release.
- Spring launch window: something live by end of February.

## Evidence we already have
In `inputs/`: six customer interviews, Q3 contact reasons, Sep upgrade hub funnel, app reviews, competitor desk research, notes from the Ops analyst.

## What I'm unsure about
- How many settlement callers actually want to upgrade versus just want to know.
- Whether showing the figure online makes people pay off and leave.
- Whether we can show trade-in value at the same point.

## Out of scope (for now)
Changes to the credit decision itself. End-of-contract upgrades (already self-serve).
