# Customer interviews: early upgrade (fictional)

Six 30-minute calls, 8-15 Sep 2025. Recruited from customers who visited the upgrade hub mid-contract in August and did not upgrade. Notes taken by the BA; quotes are verbatim from the notes.

## P1 - 34, 14 months into 24, iPhone
- Wanted the new model at launch. Hub said "call us for your settlement figure". Didn't call: "I'm not sitting on hold for 40 minutes to find out a number."
- Bought the new phone outright from a retailer instead, kept Northwind SIM. Says he'd have stayed on FlexPlan if he'd known the number.

## P2 - 52, 19 months into 36, Samsung
- Called. Waited 25 minutes. Got the figure but "the agent couldn't tell me what my old phone was worth, so I still didn't know what it would cost me."
- Worried that upgrading means "another credit check, and I'm remortgaging next year".

## P3 - 27, 9 months into 24, Pixel
- Screen cracked, wanted to upgrade instead of repairing. "Just show me what I owe. My bank shows me my loan balance, why can't you?"
- Asked if she could "just add what's left onto the new plan".

## P4 - 41, 20 months into 24, iPhone
- Didn't realise he was 4 months from the end. "If I'd known I was that close I'd have just waited."
- Would use the app if it showed the balance.

## P5 - 38, 12 months into 36, Samsung
- Lost hours at work. Was looking at the hub to see if she could get a cheaper phone and lower payments. Didn't want to upgrade, wanted to pay less.
- "Every page is about getting a new phone. Nothing about what to do if you're struggling."

## P6 - 23, 6 months into 24, iPhone
- Saw a competitor deal on social media. Wanted to compare. "I needed the payoff figure to work out if switching made sense. Couldn't get it without calling, so I didn't bother."
- Also wanted to roll the balance into a new plan.

## BA summary tally
| Theme | Participants |
|---|---|
| Can't see what they owe without calling | P1, P2, P3, P4, P6 |
| Calling is a barrier | P1, P2, P6 |
| Want trade-in value alongside the figure | P2, P6 |
| Credit check worry | P2 |
| Want to roll the balance into a new plan | P3, P6 |
| Financial difficulty, not upgrade intent | P5 |
| Didn't know how far through the contract they were | P4 |
