## Stakeholder update (exec, 150 words max)
**Subject: Settlement figure online: A/B test starts 26 Feb**

Mid-contract customers can't see what they owe without calling, and most don't call. From 26 Feb, half of them will see their settlement figure online, with a link to upgrade and a clear route to help if they're struggling.

We'll run the test for four weeks and read out on 27 Apr, once the 30-day upgrade window has closed. Success is the mid-contract upgrade rate moving from 5.0% toward 6.5%, with settlement calls down and no rise in customers paying off and leaving.

Risks: Compliance copy is the last thing on the critical path. We have a kill switch if anything looks wrong.

Ask: none right now. I'll share early numbers at sprint review on 12 Mar.

## Team / internal release note
From 16 Feb, staff on FlexPlan can see their settlement figure in their online account and on the upgrade hub. From 26 Feb, half of mid-contract customers will too. If a customer quotes a figure you can't see in the CRM, generate one as usual; the CRM link comes in March. Questions to #flexplan-settlement.

## Customer support briefing
- **What customers see:** a settlement figure, valid-until date and months left, if they're more than 3 months from the end and not in arrears or dispute.
- **Likely questions**
  - *"Why is it different from what I was told last month?"* The figure falls as you make payments; each one is valid until the date shown.
  - *"Why can't I see a figure?"* Arrears, a dispute, or under 3 months left. Generate one as usual.
  - *"Does looking at it affect my credit?"* No. Viewing a figure doesn't involve a credit check.
- **Customers in difficulty:** use the existing financial support process. Don't suggest an upgrade.
- **New contact reason:** "Viewed figure online". Please use it, it's how we measure the test.

## Sprint review talking points
- What we learned in refinement: the Agreement Service already returns figures in real time, which saved a sprint.
- What we chose not to do: roll-over of balances. Why: customer-harm risk, needs Credit Risk first.
- What we'll show next time: Phase 0 staff results and error rates.
